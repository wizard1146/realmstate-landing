Landing page: animation options

Four prototypes of the same page, each with its own motion. All of them stop moving when your system asks for reduced motion, pause when the tab or the hero is out of sight, and work in light and dark. The live page is realmstate.net.

  1. A. Embers — Slow gold embers drift up through the hero; the countdown's digits roll when they change; buttons catch a soft gold sheen on hover.
  2. B. The ring turns — A faint War Ring turns behind the wordmark: 91 states around a circle, wars flaring and fading across it, ceasefires dashed.
  3. B2. Ring and glass — B reworked: hair-thin wars and ceasefires drawn now and then, points of light waking on the rim and inside, faint shattered stained glass within the ring.
  4. C. Illuminated — Like an illuminated page: the wordmark inks in letter by letter, the gold rule draws across, the badge's diamond glows, and the sections and step diamonds turn into place as you scroll.
  5. D. Banners and land — A land of hills, peaks and a hold drifts in parallax under a hanging banner bearing the wordmark; the facts count up as they come into view.
