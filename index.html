How it works

  1. Found your house now

    Sign in with Google, name your house, and choose a race and a personality. Each one changes how you play.

  2. Nothing happens before the start

    Your house waits safely until the countdown ends, so there's no head start to lose by joining late in registration.

  3. The world moves in ticks

    Every 5 minutes a tick falls: gold and food come in, buildings finish, armies march home. Plan between them.

  4. Robots keep the world full

    Robot houses fill the empty seats in every state. When a person joins, a robot steps aside, and robots vote for people to lead.

  5. States choose leaders

    The houses of a state vote for a leader, who sets the tax, runs the treasury and decides on war.

  6. War and the market

    Attack for land, spy, work rites, and trade the materials your realm makes for the ones it doesn't.

Build your own front end

The game's screens are an ordinary website that uses the same public API anyone can. Fork it, change it, host it anywhere, and play the live game with it. Nothing about the official one is special.
